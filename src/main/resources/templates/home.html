How Campus PMS Works

A step-by-step transparent pathway for everyone involved in campus recruitment

For Students

  • Profile & Resume: Register and achieve 100% profile completeness with PDF resume upload.
  • Skill Match & Eligibility: View automatic skill match percentages and eligibility criteria checks.
  • Application Tracking: Track stages from Applied to Interview Schedule and Selection badges.

For Companies

  • Self-Registration: Register your company details and wait for quick officer verification.
  • Post Openings: Create Full-Time, Internship, and PPO job postings with specific criteria.
  • Candidate Management: Review resumes, shortlist candidates, schedule interviews, and export CSV.

For Officer

  • Verification & Approvals: Authorize companies and approve job descriptions before they go live.
  • Announcements & Placements: Broadcast college updates and mark selected students as Placed.
  • Analytics & Reports: Generate comprehensive department charts and download official CSV reports.
